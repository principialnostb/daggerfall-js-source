Cloak / leg triangle-intersection viewer

Three orthographic projections of captured geometry. Drag to pan; mouse wheel to zoom. This is not the game's camera or material renderer. Highlighted triangles are examples stored by the report (up to 100 pairs); the total hit count is shown separately.

Legend: blue = cloak; orange = leg/trousers; red = cloak triangles in hit pairs; purple = body triangles in hit pairs.
