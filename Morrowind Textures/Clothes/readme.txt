     1. Description
     
*****************************************************************
This mod is a follow up of my previous mod "Aleanne Armor and Clothes". You will need that one in order to make this work. It consist of various pieces of armor and clothes, most are a bit skimpy, so consider yourself warned. All armors and clothes id begins with the string "_ale_" and are listed in chapter 5 of this document.




Aleanne is still in the Tel Vos Services Tower. So far, she didn't make enough money to get herself her own shop. Maybe next time if you're generous enough with your drakes. She now sell the following new outfits :

ARMORS

- Volliandrium Armor (don't ask me what kind of metal it is, it's the name of a Shadowbane armor and I added a "i" somewhere in it because I think it sounded better...). This armor comes in 6 colors : silver, gold, dark red, dark blue, dark green and black. For each color, there is a set of light, medium and heavy armor. The breastplate reflect the level of "protectiveness" of the set (yeah I know, it doest looks very protective at the first glance). AR is 55 for the light and medium armor, 60 for the heavy variation. It's quite pricey, so you may have to cheat (I would :P) or sell a lot of deadric stuff.

- Ceremonial Armor. A little bit of ancient Greek inspiration. The front breastplate design was found somewhere on the net and tweaked to fit the mesh. Other parts are from me. AR 65 medium armor and flagged "legion uniform".

- Princess Armor. Nivea posted a drawing of it. Added some patterns here and there that weren't in the original design, hope she doesn't mind :). Phijama made a nice green/blue version of it to go with his headdress. AR 45 light armor.

- Hardened leather boots and cuirass. Black leather armor for the stealthy thieves and assassins out there. You know who you are. AR 35 light armor.

CLOTHES

- Black leather boots and black leather harness. Similar to the hardened leather armor, but with even less leather. Designed for the honest person who has nothing to hide. Show it to Uncle Crassius, he might like it (heck, he might even wear it) !

- Short black boots and stockings. Name says all. Designed to work with the black leather harness, but can also work with something else.

- Chain coif, chain skirt and chain skirt with stockings. Had to do it. The version with stockings is meant to be weared with boots (Durgoth high plateau boots comes to mind)

- Black fur skirt. A retexture of the skirts of my first mod. Goes well with Andy's black coat, methinks.

- Leather Skirt. An experiment in using a real photo. Turned out so-so.

- Long and short togas. For your next wild toga party with Uncle Crassius.

- Red shorts and bra. Shiny! And because I needed some red in this plugin. Also uses real photos as a base.

- Sorceress outfit. Originated from an image posted by aninerfan808 on Psychodog Studios (http://www.paul-elisa.de/dorian/dorian_cleavenger_creation.jpg). Couldn't get the top right, so I designed an alternate one. Also made a slightly less revealing bottom. Both are included.

- Witch dress and bracers. A black dress to wear at Halloween. Find a buddy skeleton at the nearest ancestral tomb and give candies to the kids.
 

See the screenshots-1.jpg and screenshots-2.jpg files for screenshots. If there is no jpg files, then I forgot to include them. You can call me silly.

     2. Installing the plugin

*****************************************************************

The esp file "ale_clothing_v1.esp" goes into Morrowind\Data Files directory
All meshes (.nif files) goes to Morrowind\Data Files\Meshes\Aleanne directory
All textures (.bmp, .dds, .tga save icons) goes to Morrowind\Data Files\Textures\Aleanne directory
All icons goes to Morrowind\Data Files\Icons\Aleanne directory
The readme.txt and screenshots.jpg files are not needed for the plug-in to run.

The plug in has been cleaned with testools to remove the 72 evil GMST.

Altered Cells :

Toddtest (Added a test NPC, removed it after tests were completed)
Tel Vos, Service Tower (added a second chest).

*****************************************************************

     3. Dependencies

*****************************************************************

Technically, this mod is only dependant on Morrowind.esm. However, it was designed for Better Bodies v.2.1. Using another body replacer or none at all may yield funny results. You must, however, use my previous mod (ale_clothing_v0.esp) to make this one run.


*****************************************************************

     4. Issues

*****************************************************************

Enchanting alpha channeled clothes or armor may produces strange results and graphical glitches, or not. Save before enchanting.

Some ground art is not as good as it could be (skirts notably). But I'm lazy. And you should store your stuff in chests anyway, or the moths will eat it :P. I became better at ground art tho.

Clothing or armor disappearing when casting chameleon/invisibility or being under water with pixel shader on, or when viewing the character from certains angles. Those are game engine limitations. 

The paperdoll will look strange when wearing the outfits, because it's not designed to use alpha channel. Once again, it's a game engine limitation.

Aleanne (the vendor) shoes are not for sale because they need racial scripting for the skin color. I don't know if Solar released a scripted version of his shoes, you'll have to check for yourself.
