## Performance contracts

The shared texture queue, archive leases, player build priority, lazy loose-file
reads and dev URL-glob transformation remain in place. Better Bodies' prepared
bytes retain their identity; the ESM and texture caches, weapon prewarm/retries,
texture healing, and deferred reach sweeps remain wired. An unsettled graphical
hand holds only its picture, never the game's swing.

The integration's motion measurements exposed repeated whole-skeleton matrix
walks to measure feet, clear arms and turn the trunk. `skeletonSpaceMatrices`
accepts an optional fourth argument with requested bones; it composes those bones
and their ancestors with the same arithmetic, and still performs the original
full walk when omitted. The stride, arm-clearance and trunk measurements use
that option. No pose is cached across mutations. `sparsepose.test.js` compares
every returned matrix exactly with the full walk across changed poses, scales
and root spaces, and rejects reads of unrelated branches.

This is a computation reduction, with no animation, clipping, geometry or game
mechanics removed. It changes no DFU rule. The shared rig is used by all four
hosts: world, exterior, worldModes and dungeonContext; this adjustment adds no
behaviour or statements to their host functions.

baseline weapon swaps 13–46 ms, integration 14–44 ms. First builds were 1908 and
1786 ms respectively. These are observations, not timing assertions.
